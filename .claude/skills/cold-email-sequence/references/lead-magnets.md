# Lead Magnets (offer assets)

Greg-authored assets the offer-centric style draws from. For an offer email, the user picks one
or you pick the best fit for the persona. In copy, attribute the asset to Greg, describe its value
compellingly, and **ask if they want it sent** — the offer is reply-to-receive and fulfilled manually.
Do NOT put a download link or `{MAGNET_LINK}` in the copy. Mirrors `src/engine/email/leadMagnets.ts`.

`Fit` lists the persona keys (see personas.md) each asset resonates with most.

| Asset | Hook (the value to lead with) | Fit | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cohort Operations Playbook** | A step-by-step guide for running startup cohorts: onboarding, curriculum, mentoring, reporting, engagement. | eso, university, chamber, government | Playbook |
| **Program Funding Guide** | Five revenue streams ESO programs can build to fund themselves sustainably. | eso, chamber, government | Guide |
| **Five Domains Self-Audit** | A founder self-assessment across the five business domains, with the gaps that quietly stall companies. | founder, mentor | Self-Audit |
| **Term Sheets: The Two-Force Architecture** | A founder's field guide to economics and control in a priced round, with a 16-box pre-sign checklist. | founder, investor, mentor | Guide |
| **Cap Table Dynamics** | How dilution, option pools, SAFEs, and the waterfall actually work at three exit prices. | founder, investor, mentor | Guide |
| **The Founder's Field Guide to Investors** | How capital thinks: the four investor personas inside every firm and what each needs to hear. | founder, investor | Guide |
| **The Optionality Architecture** | Why most startups fail before they fail, and the architecture of decisions that quietly removes your ability to win. | founder, mentor, investor | Guide |
| **The Board Meeting Playbook** | Run the meeting before the meeting: eight board-member personas and how to walk in with the room already aligned. | founder, mentor | Playbook |
| **The 9-Dimension Founder Profile** | A print-and-write self-assessment workbook for founders and mentors to surface readiness across nine dimensions. | founder, mentor, investor | Workbook |
| **Why You Feel Lost** | A founder's field guide to ecosystem fragmentation and the exhausting work of doing it alone. | founder | Guide |
| **The Infrastructure Era** | An executive briefing on why entrepreneurship ate the last 30 years, and what comes next for every comparable sector. | partner, provider, investor, government | Briefing |
| **The 7-Stream Revenue Audit** | A board-level audit of the seven revenue streams every ESO can run, scored stream by stream. | eso, chamber, government | Self-Audit |
| **Selecting Founders in the AI Era** | A new selection rubric for when AI broke the application filter and the old signals stopped working. | eso, investor, university | Rubric |

**Picking the best fit:** match the asset's `Fit` to the persona; if several fit, choose the one whose
hook most directly answers the persona's pain (see personas.md).
